### I-245 · A step halted on `advisor_cap` after the advisor said continue: the approved drift fired again, and a used consult halted at once
- Found: 2026-11-17 · the workflow audit of the graphcalc live run (`.scio/agents/audit-B.md`) · issue #172
- Symptom: `advisor_cap` halts cost $18.88 of the graphcalc run's $30.67 spent on attempts that did not pass (five attempts: P4 a1, P4 a2, P7 a1, P26 a1, P15 a1). Every earlier one passed on a later attempt. On P15 attempt 1 (`build-graphcalc-m4-p15-a1`, 51 min, $6.49) the advisor was consulted on step S4 for `plan_drift on graphcalc/numfmt.py` and answered `continue` ("The design is right ... Keep it"). The next session fixed the regression. The verify after it halted: `advisor already consulted on step S4 (one call per step; 2/3 for this run); plan_drift on graphcalc/numfmt.py`.
- Root cause: two rules in `scio/loop/escalation.py`.
  - `detect` compared the step's diff with the plan on every failing verify and kept nothing of a ruling. The file the advisor had just approved was drift again one verify later.
  - `decide` halted on any signal once `advisor_allowed(step_id)` was false. The per-step cap (one call) refuses while the run's total has headroom, so the first signal after a consult ended the attempt, before the stall or iteration caps had any say.
- Fix:
  - `SCIOState.drift_approved` (default empty), keyed `v<plan version>:<step id>`. The `advise` node writes it with the verdict through `approve_drift`: only a `continue` on a consult whose triggers included `plan_drift` records the drifted files, as git names them. `open_drift` leaves approved files out; `detect` and the reason in `decide` both read it.
  - `decide`: a signal with the step's call used and the run's total not spent returns `debug`, keeps the triggers, and says `...; the step continues`. A spent total (a cap of 0 included) halts with the I-115 wording, unchanged. That `debug` turn is an ordinary fix attempt and stall candidate, so `stall` or `max_iters` ends a step that never improves.
  - Found by the gate before the first push: `test_the_milestone_waits_once_when_only_parked_features_remain` failed, alone as well as in the suite. A T1 worker that still asked its design question after the consult was sent to the debugger, whose turn replaced the worker's output, and the attempt ended on `stall` with no question: the feature stopped with retry, skip or stop instead of parking on the question (ADR 0020). `decide` now halts as before when the worker still reports `needs_design_decision`; only a signal with no open question continues.
  - ADR 0005 amendment (2026-11-17), a note on ADR 0006's fix-attempt paragraph, and the triage sentence in `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`.
- Sweep: `grep -rn "advisor_allowed" scio`: `decide` (changed) and `review_decision`, which returns None on a refused review and never halted. `grep -rn "drifted_files(" scio`: the definition, `open_drift` and `approve_drift`; no other caller. `grep -rn "\.triage\b" scio`: the `triage` edge and `after_verify` read only the route, and the halt question prints the decision; nothing reads a `debug` decision's triggers. `grep -rn "advisor_cap" evals scripts`: the eval task `c003_advisor_cap` runs with `advisor_calls_max: 0`, a spent total, and still halts; `scripts/metrics.py` counts the exit and needs no change.
