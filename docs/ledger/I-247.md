### I-247 · A Read outside the session's folder waited 15 to 112 min at three graphcalc merge stops
- Found: 2026-11-18 · graphcalc live run (#63) · the operator sessions at graphcalc merge stops 5, 7 and 10 (Read of graphcalc-run files)
- Symptom: at three of graphcalc's merge stops, a `Read` tool call on a file in the sibling workspace `graphcalc-run` returned long after it was made, while nothing else ran. Each time, the build was already waiting at its stop. A Bash `cat` or `tail` of the same kind of file returned at once.
  - Stop 5 (2026-11-15, the graphcalc session): `Read` of a recovery run's `stdout.log`, 56.5 min. The graphcalc session's RUN_LOG records it as a permission prompt.
  - Stop 7 (2026-11-17, the SCIO session): `Read` of `graphcalc-run/RUN_LOG.md`, 112.4 min (13:30:12 to 15:22:37 UTC). The owner had answered "merge 39 and run M8" at 13:27:17, so M8 started 1 h 52 min late.
  - Stop 10 (2026-11-17, the SCIO session): one of three parallel `Read`s of `hidden/shots/*.png`, 15.7 min (22:48:08 to 23:03:48 UTC).
  - In all, 184.6 min with the build stopped: the largest SCIO-side wait in M7 to M10 after the 193 min unseen stop of M9 (I-246).
- Root cause: Claude Code asks before the first `Read`, `Grep` or `Glob` outside the session's working directories, in every mode, auto mode included: "the first read outside the working directories, which prompts you" (https://code.claude.com/docs/en/permission-modes.md, checked 2026-11-18 by a research agent). A read-only Bash command such as `cat` or `tail` runs without that prompt (https://code.claude.com/docs/en/permissions.md#read-only-commands). Neither the SCIO session nor the graphcalc session had the other's folder among its working directories, and the prompt waits for as long as the owner is away. RUN_LOG's own improvement after stop 5 ("read with Bash, never with `Read`") was a habit with no guard, and it cannot cover screenshots, which only `Read` shows.
