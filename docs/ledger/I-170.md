### I-170 · A dashboard browser test timed out once on CI and passed on the rerun
- Found: 2026-11-07 · PR #83's CI on its head after `main` was merged in (run 42402585925, attempt 1) · the `checks` job
- Symptom: `FAILED tests/live/test_dashboard_page.py::test_the_build_view_shows_the_attempts_still_running - playwright._impl._errors.TimeoutError: Page.wait_for_selector: Timeout 10000ms exceeded.`; the rest of the job passed. The same commit passed locally (547 passed) and on CI attempt 2 (`checks` 14m19s). PR #83 changed only CLAUDE.md, a test of CLAUDE.md, the CHANGELOG and the guardrails, so the code under test was `main`'s, which had passed #85's CI.
- Root cause: a loaded runner. Chromium's render and the page's refetch after a socket hello took longer than the module's fixed waits: the page helpers' 10 s selector waits (`goto`, `settle`), and Playwright's default 5 s for an `expect`. The recurrence below was not one of the reconnect test's explicit 5 s selector waits but the `expect(...).to_have_text("gate")` after them, on that 5 s default (its message is an `expect`'s, `Locator expected to have text`). I-224 measured the load: beside the unit tests' workers, 2 of 3 shard runs timed out a browser wait. Not verified on the failing attempts themselves: their page state was not captured, which the fix now does.
- Fix: `tests/live/test_dashboard_page.py`.
  - One selector wait, `Watched.wait_for`, up to `WAIT_MS` (30 s); every selector wait in the module goes through it, the reconnect test's two socket waits with `RECONNECT_WAIT_MS` (15 s).
  - The module has its own `expect` (`Expect()` with `set_options(timeout=WAIT_MS)`), so every assertion waits up to 30 s and no other module's default changes. `_wait_until` defaults to 30 s.
  - A wait that times out inside `Watched.captured_on_timeout` (the selector waits, the Mermaid text wait, the refetch wait) saves `<test name>-<n>.png` and `<test name>-<n>.txt` (the URL, the `#conn` state, `data-refresh`, then the page's text) under the test's `tmp_path`, and adds both paths and the three state lines to the original error as notes, which pytest prints. A capture that fails is noted and never raised in the timeout's place.
  - The live-redraw test's 1.5 s and 5 s are not render waits but the behaviour under test (a held redraw comes at once once the selection is gone; a selection holds it 3 s at most). They stay, named `REDRAW_AFTER_SELECTION_MS` and `SELECTION_HOLD_CAP_MS`.
- Sweep: the other fixed 10 s browser waits (`grep -n "timeout=10_000" tests/live`): the three helpers in this file; the reconnect test's 5 s waits (`:694`, `:697`). Then `grep -rn "timeout=\|wait_for_selector\|expect(" tests/live`: in this file every hit is now a named wait or bound. Outside it, unchanged (not #104's files, listed in its report): `tests/live/test_dashboard_questions_page.py` (four 10 s waits, at lines 54, 65, 71 and 108, and 11 `expect` calls on the 5 s default), `tests/live/test_dashboard_recovery_page.py` (19 `expect` calls on the default), and `tests/live/test_dashboard_server.py` (its own `WAIT_S`).
- Recurrence, 2026-11-15: PR #132's CI run 43659791456, attempt 1, failed `tests/live/test_dashboard_page.py::test_a_dropped_socket_reconnects_and_catches_up` with `Locator expected to have text 'gate'`. That is the reconnect test's short wait named in the sweep above. Since #122's W-35 the unit and live tests share shard 3's runner. The rerun of the failed job passed. The fix (capture the page when a wait times out, or lengthen the waits) belongs to #104.
- Guard: `test_dashboard_page_waits_are_long_enough` (marked `scan`): it fails on a bare `timeout=` number under 15 000 in the file, on a selector wait outside `Watched.wait_for`, and on an `expect` or `_wait_until` default under `WAIT_MS`; on the old file it listed nine short waits. `test_a_timed_out_page_wait_saves_a_screenshot`: a selector that never appears, given 1 s, leaves a PNG and the page's text named in Playwright's own `TimeoutError`, and a failed screenshot is noted while the timeout is still raised.
