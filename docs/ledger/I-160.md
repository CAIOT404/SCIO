### I-160 · The drift check's path normaliser stripped the dot of a dot-directory
- Found: 2026-11-04 · issue #79, the S8 agent's sweep of `scio/loop/escalation.py`
- Symptom: `drifted_files(["./.github/workflows/ci.yml"], ...)` reported `github/workflows/ci.yml`
- Root cause: `_norm` ended with `p.lstrip("./")`, which strips any leading `.` and `/` characters, not the `./` prefix. Drift itself held (both sides went through it), but every reported path under `.github/`, `.scio/` or another dot-directory was wrong.
- Fix: `_norm` removes leading `./` and `/` prefixes only
- Sweep: `grep -rn 'lstrip("./")\|lstrip("/.")\|lstrip(".")' scio evals scripts`: a second site, `ProfileToolchain.is_test_path` (`scio/toolchain/profile.py:129`), read `.tests/x.py` as `tests/x.py` (fixed the same way); the other `lstrip("/")` and `lstrip("-")` calls strip one character on purpose
- Guard: `test_drift_keeps_the_dot_of_a_dot_directory`, `test_a_test_path_keeps_the_dot_of_a_dot_directory` (each fails on the old code); guardrail 50
