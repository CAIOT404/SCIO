The bottleneck audit as numbers (issue #141): `scripts/metrics.py [--days 7] [--product-root PATH] [--json] [--write]` reports SCIO's PR lead time (open to merge), the rework share (a PR with a failed CI run), the `low-risk` share, CI and gate time as median and p90, hook refusals by rule and slips by kind, and, for a product workspace, features passed per day, cost per passed feature, the share of attempts that ended at a human stop (with the failures that had none counted apart), and the time stalled at a human stop. `--write` caches them in `.scio/metrics.json`, which `scripts/session_state.py` shows with their age; the `Stop` hook's background run refreshes a cache that is missing or over 6 hours old (`--product-root` from `SCIO_METRICS_PRODUCT_ROOT`). Every finished gate appends its mode, time and verdict to `gate-runs.log` in the machine directory, and `scripts/slip.py "<kind>" "<what happened>"` counts a slip caught within the same turn with no effect in `slips.log` there, instead of a ledger entry. A refusal that the user-level and project-level push-gate hooks both logged counts once (I-231).
