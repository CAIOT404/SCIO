A feature stop's `retry` can carry the owner's answer: `retry: <answer>` records it, with the question the attempt stopped with, and every later attempt of the feature in the milestone reads both in its task, under "## The owner's answers to earlier attempts' questions". The question is the coder's own design question (on any tier), or the stop's own line when it asked none. A plain `retry` builds the same task as before, and so does `retrying ...`. graphcalc's P29 coder asked which of two rules to use, and the owner's one-sentence answer had no way to reach the next attempt (issue #137, I-228).
