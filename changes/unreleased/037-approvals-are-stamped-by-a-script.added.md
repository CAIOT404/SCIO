`uv run python scripts/approve.py "<the owner's words>" --refs "issue 140, Refs #141"` records an approval: it appends `<UTC time> | <words> | <refs>` to the main checkout's `.scio/approvals.md` (also from a worktree), with the time taken from the clock, and prints the line. It writes nothing and exits 2 when the words are empty or hold a `|` or a newline, or when any ref is not one `scripts/land.py` reads (`PR <n>`, `issue <n>`, `Refs #<n>`): the line is checked with `land.py`'s own parser first. `--note "<text>"` records a decision that approves no PR. Before, the lines were typed by hand: twice the time was hours wrong, and once the refs approved more than the owner said (Workflow v3, WP5).
